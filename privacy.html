BudgetFlow Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

BudgetFlow (“the App”) is a personal finance tool created by an individual developer. This Privacy Policy explains how the App collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use BudgetFlow and any connected financial integrations such as QuickBooks Online.

1. Information We Collect

BudgetFlow may collect the following types of information:

  • Financial Data: Transaction data, account names, categories, and related financial information retrieved through integrations such as QuickBooks Online.
  • Usage Data: Logs or metadata generated by your use of the App, such as refresh timestamps or error logs.
  • Authentication Data: OAuth tokens or credentials required to connect to QuickBooks Online or other financial services.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely for the following purposes:

  • To retrieve and display your financial data inside BudgetFlow.
  • To categorize, analyze, and visualize your financial activity.
  • To maintain secure connections with QuickBooks Online or other financial APIs.
  • To improve the App’s functionality and reliability.

3. How Your Information Is Stored

BudgetFlow stores data locally on your device or within your personal cloud storage environment (e.g., Excel files, local scripts, or your QuickBooks Online account). BudgetFlow does not operate a centralized server and does not store your financial data on any external infrastructure controlled by the developer.

4. Sharing of Information

BudgetFlow does not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties. Your data is only transmitted to services you explicitly connect, such as QuickBooks Online, and only for the purpose of retrieving or updating your financial records.

5. Security

BudgetFlow uses industry-standard OAuth flows and secure API communication. However, because the App is operated by you in your own environment, you are responsible for maintaining the security of your device, files, and credentials.

6. Third-Party Services

BudgetFlow integrates with QuickBooks Online and may integrate with other financial APIs. Your use of these services is governed by their respective privacy policies and terms.

7. Children’s Privacy

BudgetFlow is not intended for use by children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Updates will be posted on this page.
